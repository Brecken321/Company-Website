Who we are

Family-run —
and that's the point.

Our dad runs the business, and the two of us handle the design and the build. No account managers, nothing outsourced, no ticket queue. The people who answer your email are the same people who built your site.

We work with small businesses — coffee shops, shops on main street, trades, the places that run on word of mouth. Most of them already do great work. They just have a website that doesn't say so.

Meet us properly

Phone first

Easy to find

How we build

Serious tools. Human judgment.

We use AI as a coding assistant, the same way a carpenter uses a nail gun. It speeds up the repetitive part so we can spend our time on the parts that decide whether your website actually works.

  1. Step 1

    Research

    Your menu, your hours, your reviews, and what people in your area search for.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    How it looks and reads, chosen for your business. Every decision here is ours.

  3. Step 3

    Code, assisted

    An AI assistant writes the first draft. We then rewrite it until it does what we designed.

  4. Step 4

    Check

    Both of us read the whole thing and test it on real phones, tablets and laptops.

  5. Step 5

    Hand over

    You see it, you approve it, we put it online and give you every file.
