Coffeehouse & bakery Deerwood, MN 2026

Auntie M’s

A coffee shop, bakery and hand-dipped ice cream counter that has been running on Old Minnesota 6 since 1997. Six pages, built from scratch around their real menu, real hours and real address.

What we set out to do

Everything a customer asks on the phone, answered before they call.

A small shop's website has a few jobs to do: say what you sell, say when you're open, say where you are, and look appetizing doing it. Everything on this build serves one of those four.

We set the hours, address and phone number up the way Google, Apple Maps and voice assistants expect — so “is Auntie M's open right now?” gets the right answer without anyone opening the site.

Six pages, not one

Home, menu, ice cream, story, gallery and visit — each doing a job, none padded out with filler.

A menu you can read in the car

The board above the counter, retyped as a proper web page you can jump around by category.

Says when it's open

The top of every page works out whether the shop is open right now, and says so in plain words.

How this one happened

We build first, then knock on the door.

We liked the place, thought it deserved a proper website, and built the whole thing before asking anyone for anything. Then we took it over on a laptop — finished, and free to look at.
