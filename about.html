What we do

We build the whole thing, from scratch.

A lot of small business websites are made the fast way: a ready-made template and a monthly subscription. That gets you online, and for some businesses it's genuinely enough. The catch is that it's slow, it looks like a thousand other sites, and it stops being yours the day you stop paying.

We go the other way. Your site is built for your business and nothing else, which is why it ends up quick, sturdy and unlike anyone else's. When we hand it over, it's yours outright.

We're not trying to become an agency. We like it small.

What we do ourselves

  • Deciding what your site should say, and in what order
  • The whole design — how it looks and how it reads
  • Writing the words on every page
  • Reading every line of code before it goes out
  • Testing it on real phones and laptops
  • Sitting down with you and explaining it in plain English

Why this matters to you

Left to itself, an AI assistant writes things that look finished and quietly aren't — a menu that reads wrong on a phone, an address Google can't understand, a contact form that goes nowhere. You only catch that by sitting down and checking, so that's what we do.

It makes us faster, not lazier. The time it saves goes back into the research, the design and the testing — the parts that decide whether your website actually brings anyone through the door.

Fair questions

The things people ask us.

Do you use AI to build these?

Yes, as a coding assistant, and we'd rather tell you than have you find out later. It writes the first draft of the code quickly. The research, the design, the words, the testing and the final read of every line are ours — and nothing goes on your site that one of us hasn't checked.

Does that mean it's a template?

No. A template is one design sold to thousands of businesses. Yours is designed for your business and built from nothing, which is why no two of our sites look alike.

Can you use my own photos?

Please do. Real photos of your actual place beat stock images every time. If you don't have good ones, we'll say so and talk through the options.

How long does it take?

Add your usual timeline. It depends on how many pages you need and how quickly we can get your menu, hours and photos — but we'll give you a straight answer at the start.

What if I already have a website?

That's usually the easiest kind of job. We'll show you a new one next to what you've got, and you can judge for yourself whether it's worth changing.
